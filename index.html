The Event

Taipei Party Olympics 🎉

Teams (3–5 persons) collect points through challenges. Completion proof can be done by photo / video evidence or having another team vouch for you. The winner team gets a small prize 👀🏆

📌 Rules

  • Teams of 3–5 persons
  • Challenges can generally be completed once per team. Some can only be completed once overall.
  • Have fun & don't die
