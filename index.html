The Event

Taipei Party Olympics 🎉

Teams collect points through challenges. Proof of completion: photo/video evidence or having another team vouch for you. The winning team gets a small prize 👀🏆

📌 Rules

  • Teams of 3–5 persons — fill out the Google Form at the bottom of the page. Your team can have more members, but additional members won't be able to collect points or win a prize.
  • Challenges can generally be completed once per team. Some can only be completed once overall.
  • Have fun & don't die

The Challenges

Let's Get It 🏋️

Easy · 1–3 pts Medium · 4–6 pts Hard · 7–9 pts Legend · 10 pts

🍺 Alcoholic Challenges

  • The Simple Things in Life — Enjoy a beer. You must look very enjoying doing that! Can be completed up to 3 times.2 pts
  • Everybody, Shots Shots, … — Have a round of shots.3 pts
  • When in Rome… — Ask someone for an alcoholic drink recommendation. Drink it. You may not influence their suggestion.4 pts
  • Heavy-weight — Empty a can of strong beer.4 pts
  • Horse Race — Challenge another team. 2 members per team participate: one is the rider, one is the horse. The rider jumps onto the horse's back and you race to a destination of your choice. You must empty a drink before arriving — but as a responsible rider, you cannot drink while the horse is moving. Up to 8 points can be earned.+4 / −2 pts
  • Busdriver — Play a round of the drinking game "Bus Driver".5 pts
  • The Lion Drinks Tonight — One teammate empties a drink while the rest sing "The Lion Sleeps Tonight".6 pts
  • Friend or Foe? — Find another team. Both teams secretly mix a drink for the other. Exchange and decide: drink it for points, or don't. Your decision only affects your own team.7 pts
  • What Are They Doing? — 4-person challenge. One person holds your arms, one holds your legs, and the 4th person pours alcohol into your mouth.7 pts
  • Upside-Down — Have a drink while being upside down. You'll figure out how.7 pts
  • Stand United — Get a stranger to join you for at least one drink.10 pts ★
  • Stay Healthy — Buy a salad. Buy a beer. Mix them. Eat up.10 pts ★

🚫🍺 Non-Alcoholic Challenges

  • Cheers to a Good Evening! — Clink glasses with your whole team.1 pt
  • Team Ready! — Take a group photo.1 pt
  • Best Snack — Find "Taiwan's Nr.1 Snack".1 pt
  • Discount Lover — Buy something at a discount.2 pts
  • Good Boy — Place Guai Guai on top of an electronic device (not your phone).2 pts
  • KTV Enjoyer — Sing something out loud!3 pts
  • Retiree — Practice some Taiji.3 pts
  • The Tizian — Win something at a claw machine. Can be completed up to 2 times.4 pts
  • Spread Some Love — Give someone outside your team a hug.4 pts
  • Dance Monkey, Dance! — Do a dance in public. There needs to be music — either from your phone or your teammates making the sound effects.4 pts
  • Stinky Gang — Buy some stinky tofu and eat it. Every team member must have a bite.4 pts
  • Language Enjoyer — Ask a Taiwanese person what Gāngjiāo is.5 pts
  • Playboy / Playgirl — Have two people kiss you on the cheeks at the same time.5 pts
  • Ha-Ha-Ha-Ha — Find another team. One member each fills their mouth with water; the other team tries to make them laugh. Whoever laughs first, loses. Only the winner gets points. Up to 10 points can be earned.6 pts
  • Just Exchange Student Things — Give someone a passionate kiss. Consensually, ofc.6 pts
  • Boba Addict — Be the first team to buy a bubble tea. Only the first team gets points for this one.6 pts
  • I Am The Stampbook — Get a stamp on your forehead. Any stamp counts.7 pts
  • KTV Lunatic — Sing something out loud inside a convenience store. Does not automatically count for KTV Enjoyer.7 pts
  • Comedian — Make a stranger laugh. Can be completed twice.7 pts
  • Spread Some Kindness — Buy something for a convenience store worker.8 pts
  • King of Convenience Stores — First team to buy from three of: 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Hi-Life, OKmart.10 pts ★
  • Twerk-a-holic — Do some wall twerking in 3 different places.10 pts ★

⚡ Other Challenges

  • 6-7 — If you spot another team and do the 6-7 gesture, at least one of their members must join in. Otherwise they lose 1 point.0 / −1 pt
  • Apes Together Strong — If you spot another team and make monkey noises, at least one of their members must join in. Otherwise they lose 2 points.0 / −2 pts
